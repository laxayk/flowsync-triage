# Sign-in, SSO and invites
Invite links expire after 7 days; an admin can resend one from Settings, Members. SSO (SAML) is available on the Business plan. If SSO sign-in fails with an assertion error, check that the identity provider sends the email attribute and that the ACS URL matches the one in Settings, Security.
Password resets are sent to the account email and expire after 30 minutes.
