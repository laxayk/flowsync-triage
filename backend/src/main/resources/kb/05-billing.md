# Billing, invoices and refunds
Invoices are emailed to the billing contact on the 1st of each month and can be downloaded from Settings, Billing. Plans renew monthly or yearly. A charge made twice in the same period is refunded in 5 to 7 business days once finance confirms it; support cannot issue refunds directly.
To change the tax ID or billing email, an account admin edits it under Settings, Billing.
