# Changing plan or cancelling
plan: all
tags: upgrade, downgrade, cancel, plan change
reviewed: 2026-10-13

## Upgrade or downgrade
An account admin can change plan under Settings, Billing. Upgrades apply immediately. Downgrades apply at the next renewal and are blocked if the account is over the lower plan's connector limit.

## Cancelling
An admin can cancel under Settings, Billing. Syncs stop at the end of the paid period. Data in your connected tools is not deleted by FlowSync.
