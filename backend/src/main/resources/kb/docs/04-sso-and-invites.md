# Sign-in, SSO and invites
plan: all
tags: sso, saml, okta, azure ad, identity provider, invite, password reset
reviewed: 2026-10-13

## Invites
Invite links expire after 7 days. An admin can resend one from Settings, Members.

## SSO
SSO (SAML) is available on the Business and Enterprise plans. If SSO sign-in fails with an assertion error, check that the identity provider sends the email attribute and that the ACS URL matches the one in Settings, Security.

## Password resets
Password resets are sent to the account email and expire after 30 minutes.
