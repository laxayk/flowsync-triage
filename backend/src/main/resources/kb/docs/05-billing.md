# Billing, invoices and refunds
plan: all
tags: billing, invoice, refund, double charge, tax id
reviewed: 2026-10-13

## Invoices
Invoices are emailed to the billing contact on the 1st of each month and can be downloaded from Settings, Billing. Plans renew monthly or yearly.

## Double charges and refunds
A charge made twice in the same period is refunded in 5 to 7 business days once finance confirms it. Support cannot issue refunds directly.

## Tax ID and billing email
An account admin edits the tax ID or billing email under Settings, Billing.
