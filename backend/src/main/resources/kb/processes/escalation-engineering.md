# Escalation: Engineering (bugs and wrong behaviour)
Owner team: Engineering. Triage within 1 business day, same day for Enterprise customers or any case with possible data loss.
Before handing over, collect the connector name, run id, time window, and whether the problem began after a mapping change. Ask the customer for missing items.
Data-integrity cases (missing or duplicated rows) are treated as P2 or higher and the customer success manager is told the same day.
