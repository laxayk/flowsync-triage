# What FlowSync is
plan: all
tags: overview, getting started, connectors
reviewed: 2026-10-13

## Overview
FlowSync is a no-code service that keeps records in step between business tools. You connect a source and a target, map fields, pick a schedule, and FlowSync runs the sync and shows each run's result. FlowSync is a fictional demo product used to test this support agent.

## Supported connectors
There are four connectors: Salesforce, HubSpot, Postgres and Google Sheets. Any of them can be a source or a target, except that Google Sheets is not offered as a source on the Starter plan. There is no connector for Excel, MySQL, Snowflake or other tools.

## Setup in four steps
Connect a source, connect a target, map fields, then choose a schedule and turn the sync on.
