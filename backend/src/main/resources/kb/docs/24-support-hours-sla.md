## Support hours
Starter and Pro: weekdays, 9 to 18 in the customer's region, email only. Business: weekdays with a 4 business hour first response. Enterprise: 24x7 with first response within 1 hour for P1 and P2.

## Reporting an incident
For an outage or data problem, reply to the support email with the connector name and run id.
